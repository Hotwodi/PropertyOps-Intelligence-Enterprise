Overview

PropertyOps Intelligence Enterprise is a comprehensive commercial real estate management module for Odoo 18. It combines AI-assisted lease abstraction, CAM/NNN reconciliation, rent escalation management, owner reporting, vacancy risk forecasting, and an arrears/collections workflow into a single integrated application.

Key Features

Portfolio Dashboard

Track occupancy, rent roll, delinquencies, maintenance backlog, NOI proxy and AI vacancy forecasts across your entire portfolio.

Lease Abstraction

AI-assisted extraction of key dates, rent terms, escalation clauses, renewal options, deposits and CAM terms from lease documents with confidence scoring and review workflow.

CAM / NNN Reconciliation

Reconcile common-area maintenance expenses against tenant shares, track variances and manage disputes through a draft-to-closed workflow.

Escalation Rules

Support for fixed, CPI-index, step-percentage and step-amount escalation types with auto-apply and next-escalation scheduling.

Owner Reporting

Generate period-based owner statements with occupancy, rent collected, delinquencies, maintenance spend, NOI, capex and AI performance scores.

Vacancy Timeline

Per-unit vacancy risk tracking with AI renewal probability, market demand indicators, suggested actions and risk-level scoring.

Arrears Workflow

Collections pipeline from first reminder through legal action with AI collection probability scoring and escalation management.

Integrated & Extensible

Built on Odoo 18 native patterns with mail-thread integration, chatter on key records and standard security groups.
